Who We Are

Fictional World Library is a fiction-reading platform built around the connection between stories and the people who love them. We aim to make discovering a new book, returning to a favourite story and keeping track of your reading straightforward and enjoyable.

We are developing a space where readers can explore different genres, follow stories, organise their libraries and discover new authors. We also want writers to have a clear path to learn about submissions, publishing opportunities and the terms under which their work may be published.

Our Approach to Reading

We want the reading experience to feel comfortable, easy to navigate and focused on the story. Our planned reader features include a personal library, reading progress, notifications, account settings and a wallet for eligible paid features.

Some books or chapters may require coins or an eligible subscription. The applicable price and access conditions should be displayed before a reader makes a purchase. Paid features are subject to successful payment verification.

Our Approach to Authors

Stories begin with people who imagine something that does not yet exist. We want to make our publishing requirements understandable so writers can consider whether the platform and a proposed contract are right for them.

Author applications, exclusive and non-exclusive arrangements, revenue shares, bonuses and payment schedules are subject to the relevant signed agreement. Writers should review all contract terms carefully before signing.
