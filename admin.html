Before you apply

Short stories

12–25 chapters, with 600–1,000 words per chapter. Submit at least 5 completed chapters. Review takes 3–7 working days.

Long stories

30–60 chapters, with 600–1,000 words per chapter. Submit at least 10 completed chapters. Review takes 3–7 working days.

Exclusive contract

Short story: $30 signing bonus and $20 completion bonus. Long story: $50 signing bonus and $30 completion bonus. Exclusive rights apply for 10 years.

Non-exclusive contract

60% revenue share. Milestone rewards may reach $100 for short stories or $200 for long stories, subject to the agreed chapter and revenue requirements.

Payments

Payments are made on the 25th of each month for the previous month's earnings. Minimum withdrawal is $50. Naira payments use Nigerian bank accounts; USD payments use a Grey or Raenest Tag.

Confirm the current contract and guidelines before submitting.
