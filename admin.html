About Fictional World Library

Fictional World Library is a digital reading platform created to bring readers and storytellers together through engaging fiction.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make discovering stories enjoyable, give readers a convenient place to follow their favourite books, and provide authors with opportunities to share their work.

What We Offer

Readers can explore genres, follow ongoing stories, discover completed books, and build a personal library. Eligible chapters may be available free or through coin-based unlocking.

For Authors

Writers can explore our author programme and review its current requirements before submitting their work.

Contact: fictionalworldlibary@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fictional World Library handles information when readers use the platform.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, information may include your email address, display name, reader account identifier, profile picture, gender if supplied, reading progress, library activity, coin balance, transaction records, subscription status, and support correspondence.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate accounts, provide reading features, save library activity, process purchases, maintain subscriptions, investigate payment issues, respond to support requests, and protect the platform.

Payments

Payments may be processed through Paystack. Payment details are handled according to the payment provider's systems and applicable requirements. Fictional World Library should not collect or store card security codes in its own database.

Service Providers

We may rely on service providers for database hosting, authentication, image storage, website hosting, payment processing, and operational security. Information should only be shared as necessary for their functions and under applicable terms.

Cookies and Technical Data

Authentication sessions and necessary browser storage may be used to keep you signed in and operate the platform. Hosting and security systems may also process technical request information.

Data Security and Retention

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Records may be retained for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and maintain financial records.

Your Choices

You can manage available profile information and contact support about access, correction, or deletion requests. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, financial, or legal purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact fictionalworldlibary@gmail.com.
