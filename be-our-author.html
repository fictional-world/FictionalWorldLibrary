01. Choose Your Story Length

Short Story

Short Fiction

Suitable for focused stories with a satisfying beginning and ending.

  • 12–25 chapters.
  • 600–1,000 words per chapter.
  • At least 5 completed chapters for an application.
  • Remaining chapters are due within 2 weeks after signing unless another deadline is agreed in writing.
Long Story

Long Fiction

Suitable for extended plots, character development, and continuing conflict.

  • 30–60 chapters.
  • 600–1,000 words per chapter.
  • At least 10 completed chapters for an application.
  • Remaining chapters are due within 4–5 weeks after signing unless another deadline is agreed in writing.

02. Submission and Review

  1. Prepare an original story that you have the right to submit.
  2. Complete the required number of chapters for your chosen category.
  3. Contact the author team to confirm the current submission procedure and required materials.
  4. Our team will review a complete application, normally within 3–7 working days.
  5. If your story is accepted, review the proposed contract, rights, payment terms, and deadlines before signing.

Review times are estimates, not a guarantee of acceptance. Please do not submit work that infringes another person's rights.

04. Deadlines and Completion

Before signing, confirm the final chapter count, delivery schedule, editing requirements, revision process, and completion criteria.

Short-story and long-story deadlines are described above. Any change to an agreed deadline should be approved in writing by the relevant representative.

A completion bonus, where offered, is payable only after the applicable completion requirements have been met and verified.

05. Payments and Withdrawals

  • Scheduled monthly payment date: the 25th of each month for the previous month's eligible earnings.
  • Minimum withdrawal threshold: $50 equivalent, subject to the applicable payment method and contract.
  • For naira payments, an eligible Nigerian bank account may be used.
  • For dollar payments, an eligible Grey or Raenest account or tag may be used, subject to provider availability and verification.

Processing times, currency conversion, transfer fees, eligibility, and required verification may affect the amount received and when it arrives. Confirm the current payment procedure with the author team.

06. What We Expect From Our Authors

  • Submit original work that you have the right to publish.
  • Follow the agreed chapter length, story structure, and deadlines.
  • Respond professionally to editorial feedback and revision requests.
  • Do not plagiarise or misrepresent authorship.
  • Read the entire contract before signing.
  • Keep your contact and payment information accurate and secure.
