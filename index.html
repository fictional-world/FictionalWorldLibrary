Be Our Author

Bring your stories to readers around the world. Fictional World Library welcomes original fiction with compelling characters, strong plots, and engaging chapters.

Story requirements

Chapter length: 600–1,000 words per chapter.

Short stories: 12–25 chapters. Submit at least 5 completed chapters when applying.

Long stories: 30–60 chapters. Submit at least 10 completed chapters when applying.

Reviews normally take 3–7 working days. Deadlines for remaining chapters must be agreed upon in writing.

Exclusive contract

Short story: $30 signing bonus and $20 completion bonus.

Long story: $50 signing bonus and $30 completion bonus.

Exclusivity, rights, eligibility and payment conditions must be stated in the signed contract.

Non-exclusive contract

Revenue share: 60%, subject to the signed agreement.

Short story milestone: Up to $100, subject to publishing at least 25 chapters and reaching $50 qualifying revenue within 3 months of signing.

Long story milestone: Up to $200, subject to publishing at least 60 chapters and reaching $50 qualifying revenue within 3 months of signing.

Contract and deadline

Read your contract carefully before signing. Chapter schedules, rights, bonuses, revenue calculations, and any exclusivity restrictions should be confirmed in writing.

Any deadline extension must be agreed upon with the platform.

After publishing

Payments are scheduled for the 25th of each month for the previous month's eligible earnings. The minimum withdrawal is $50. Available payout methods depend on currency and account eligibility.

For naira payments, use an eligible Nigerian bank account. For dollar payments, an eligible Grey or Raenest account may be used where supported.
